# Learner Profile

## Initial Idea
Goal-to-execution planning application for students and independent builders who struggle to turn a vague ambition into realistic daily work. Transforms goal, deadline, available time, progress, and constraints into an achievable plan with tasks, dependencies, priorities, estimated effort, and completion criteria. Includes replanning based on actual completion, adjusting constraints, without faking completion or moving deadlines secretly.

## Demonstrated Technical and Agent Experience
Very experienced with coding, testing, and AI coding agents.

## Planning-Workflow Experience
Familiar with planning-first and spec-driven development.

## Desired Learning Outcome
Produce a fully working PoC for the Build With AI: Basics hackathon under a tight deadline and requirements. Focus on adapting replanning rules correctly with dependencies and time constraints.

## Learning Moments
Not established yet.

## Interests, Inspirations, and Goals
To build a practical execution planner that provides structured tasks, milestone tracking, explicit capacity allocations and realistic replanning without silently dropping requirements. The focus is to make it useful for real execution, not just generic advice generation.
